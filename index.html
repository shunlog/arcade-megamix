Arcade Megamix

Five classic arcade games in one, switching at an ever faster rate.
Survive as long as you can and rack up the highest score.

  Snake      - Arrows to steer. Turns are buffered, so you can queue up to 3 at once.
  Asteroids  - Left / Right to rotate, Up to thrust, Space to fire.
  Pong       - Up / Down to move the left paddle.
  Tron       - Arrows to steer your light cycle.
  Pac-Man    - Arrows to move.

P or Esc pauses. Q quits to the menu. R restarts after a game over.
